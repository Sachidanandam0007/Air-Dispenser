About us

From the first sketch to the final stitch

Fashion Queens Designing Academy helps students turn ideas into finished garments. Learn step by step and build the confidence to work in fashion or start your own boutique.

Our Courses

What you will learn

Fashion illustration

Draw figures and bring design ideas to paper.

Pattern making

Take measurements and draft patterns that fit.

Garment construction

Stitch blouses, kurtis and dresses.

Embroidery and aari work

Add hand and machine detailing.

Fabric knowledge

Choose the right fabric, colour and drape.

Boutique basics

Plan, price and promote your own line.
