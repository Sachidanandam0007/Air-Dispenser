AboutUs

Fashion Queens Designing Academy is a fashion designing institute opposite the Gandhipuram bus stand in Coimbatore. We help students turn a sketch into a finished garment.

From the first drawing to the final stitch, you learn the craft step by step and build the confidence to work in fashion or start your own label.

CREATIVITYSketch and plan designs that feel like you.
SKILLCut, stitch and finish garments with care.
EMPOWERMENTBe ready for a career or your own boutique.
